# Governance

Aval is at an early stage and is led by its founder, who acts as the project lead. As the
community grows, decision-making will move to a team of maintainers, following the path of
established open-source language projects.

## Decisions

- Changes to the language and tools are proposed as OpenSpec changes (see
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)) and discussed in public pull requests.
- Maintainers aim for consensus. When it cannot be reached, the project lead decides and explains
  the decision in writing.
- Accepted requirements and locked specifications change only with a maintainer's approval.
